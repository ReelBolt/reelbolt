# Make a promo video, with or without a voiceover

**Goal:** make a short promo of your product from its code, and choose whether a voice reads it out.

## Recipe: Product promo, your way

**Inputs.** A code archive or repository in the project. On the app page you set what the video should show (notes),
who it is for, the call to action, language, length and shape, and the **Spoken voiceover** switch. Under **More options**
you choose the narrator voice, the tone, the voice model (when the voiceover is on), and the script and video builder
models. Setting meanings are in [Run settings](run-settings.md).

**What it costs.** It uses credits, and a voice when the voiceover is on. The run page shows the price before you start,
and it follows the switch: with the voiceover off, the narration step does not run and is not charged.

**What you get.** One promo video of your product with on-screen text and captions written for you. With the voiceover on, it is narrated.

**If it fails.**

- The voiceover is on and the run stops at the narration: add a voice provider to your workspace, or switch the voiceover off.
- The promo misses your audience: fill in **Who it is for** and run again.
- Nothing about your code is read: check that the project has a code archive or a connected repository.

**Permission to send media.** This recipe does not send your pictures or videos to a provider. If a step ever needs that,
it stays off until you switch it on yourself in the app; the assistant cannot do it for you.

See also [Promo video from your code](promo-from-code.md), the same promo without the voiceover switch.
