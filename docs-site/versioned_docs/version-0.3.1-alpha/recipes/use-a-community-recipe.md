# Use a community recipe

**Goal:** find a recipe made by someone else and use it in your own project.

## Browse without an account

The community gallery is open to everyone, on the ReelBolt site and inside the app (choose **Community**, or **Browse community recipes** from your apps). You can search, filter by **Category**, **Language**, **Cost** and **Step type**, and sort by **Newest**, **Most copied** or **Top rated**. Each recipe shows a short preview video, its steps, its prompts and a note about what it costs to run.

## Check the prompts first

Recipes are written by other people. Before you copy one, read **What it does** and **Text this recipe sends to AI models** on the recipe page. A copy does what the recipe says, not what you hope it says.

## Copy a recipe

1. Sign in with a verified email address.
2. Open the recipe and choose **Copy to my project**.
3. Pick the project, optionally change the name, then choose **Copy**.

The copy is an ordinary app in your project. Choose **Open the app** to see it. Nothing runs and nothing is charged when you copy.

## What you will need

The recipe page lists this under **What you will need**.

A copy can ask you to fill in what the author could not share: an AI connection, a voice, or a file. The copy lists each one under **Needs** with the step it belongs to. The recipe also says whether it uses credits, a voice or generated shots. Allowing your footage to be sent to outside services is switched off in every copy; switch it on yourself if a step needs it.

## Your copy is yours

The copy has no link back to the recipe. If the author later changes, unpublishes or loses the recipe, your copy stays exactly as it is. Copying again with the same name in the same project returns the copy you already have.
