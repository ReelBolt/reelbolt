# Share a recipe

**Goal:** put one of your apps in the community gallery, where anyone can watch a short preview and copy the recipe into their own project.

## Before you start

- Your email address must be verified.
- The app must be made of ordinary steps. A recipe that uses your own custom agents, points at a result of one of your earlier runs, or uses a timeline render cannot be shared. ReelBolt tells you the reason in words and which step it concerns.
- You need a finished video from the project to use as the preview.

## Publish

1. Open the app you want to share and choose **Share as recipe**. The **Share as a community recipe** window opens and checks what can be shared.
2. Read the list of what will be removed before the recipe goes public. See [What is removed from a recipe you share](./what-is-removed-from-a-shared-recipe.md).
3. Give the recipe a name, a short description, a category and a language, and pick a finished video under **Preview video**.
4. Check **Author name shown**. It starts as your account name and you can change it for this recipe. It is the only thing about you that people see.
5. Under **Before you share**, read the text and tick **I have read this and I agree**. It says: "Anyone may view, copy and adapt this recipe and show its preview. You confirm you have the right to publish the preview and everything in it. You can unpublish it at any time; copies already made stay."
6. Choose **Share recipe**.

## What happens next

ReelBolt prepares the preview and checks the text and the preview. If everything passes and you have shared a recipe before, or your workspace is on a paid plan, the recipe appears in the gallery. Otherwise it waits for a person on the ReelBolt team to look at it, and you see its status under **My recipes**. See [Review, removal and your copies](./review-removal-and-your-copies.md).

## Limits

- 5 publishes per day for each person.
- 50 live recipes for each workspace.

Your recipes are listed under **My recipes** in the Community area of the app.

## Change or take down a recipe

Sharing again from an app creates a new version, which is checked in the same way. Choose **Unpublish** next to the recipe in **My recipes** at any time. Copies that people already made stay with them.
