# Rate and report a recipe

## Rate a recipe

Under **Rate this recipe**, choose from one to five stars once your workspace has copied it and a run of that copy has finished successfully. Each account rates a recipe once, and you can change your rating. There are no written reviews.

The average appears only after a recipe has at least 5 ratings. The usage number is how many different workspaces copied the recipe.

## Report a recipe

Report a recipe that is illegal, sexual, copied from someone else, spam, misleading, or wrong for another reason.

1. Open the recipe.
2. Choose **Report this recipe** and pick the reason: illegal or harmful, sexual content, uses someone else's work, spam or an advert, misleading, or something else.
3. Add details if it helps, then choose **Send report**. The details are limited to 500 characters.

You need a verified email address and can send up to 10 reports a day. The author never sees who reported.

When several different people report the same recipe, it is hidden from the gallery until a person on the ReelBolt team has looked at it. To ask for a takedown yourself, see [Review, removal and your copies](./review-removal-and-your-copies.md).
