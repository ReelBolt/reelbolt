# Run settings (the brief)

The settings on a recipe's run page together make up the **brief** for that run. The page shows
only the settings the recipe actually uses, so a recipe without narration has no narration
setting. Leave a setting empty to say you have no preference.

## Language

The language of everything the viewer reads or hears: on-screen text, captions and narration.
Pick a language, or leave it on auto. A recipe that edits footage also has **Spoken language**,
which is the language people speak in your clips; it helps with finding the words in them.

## Length

A target in seconds, between 5 and 600. It is a target, not a guarantee: the result lands near it,
and a short clip of footage cannot be stretched past what you filmed.

## Shape

The frame of the video: vertical (9:16), square (1:1), landscape (16:9) or portrait (4:5). Pick it
before you run. A finished video cannot be changed to another shape afterwards.

## Narration

Whether the video is narrated and by which voice. Choose **Narrated** and a voice you have
consent for. This setting appears only on recipes that can speak. See [Add a voiceover](voiceover.md).

## Style

The editing style and tone: pace, cuts, captions and mood. A recipe can suggest a style, shown
as a chip; you can change it.

## Design

Which brand the video is built in. Use your project's approved design (the default), or choose
none to run without brand colours and fonts. See [Design system](../design-system.md).

## Files

The files a recipe works on: your clips, music, pictures or a code archive. Pick them from the
project's files. A recipe that needs a file you have not uploaded says so above **Run**.

## Notes

Free text for anything the other settings do not cover: who the video is for, what it should
show, what to keep. When the notes disagree with a setting, the setting wins.

## Recipe fields

Where your workspace has them, some recipes also ask for a few fields of their own, each with an example in the box.
The ones most people change are shown; the rest sit under **More options**, closed until you open them. A field you
leave empty keeps the recipe's own setting.

| Recipe | Fields | Under More options |
|---|---|---|
| Promo video from your code, lighter, and in your brand | **Call to action** (the last thing the video asks people to do) | **Script model**, **Video builder model** |
| Promo with AI backgrounds | **Call to action**, **Background look** (the colours and mood of the backgrounds) | **AI background model**, **Script model**, **Video builder model** |
| Narrated feature spotlight, launch trailer, narrated story | **Who it is for**; the narrated story also has **Captions** | |
| Highlight edit from footage (also with rooms, graphics, grade or sound effects), testimonial reel | **Captions** (off by default: the words spoken in your clips, which are transcribed for it) | **Ending fade** (on by default) |
| Footage edit with narration and captions, dub | **Captions** (on by default) | **Ending fade** |
| Footage edit with background music | | **Music volume** (empty lets the editor choose), **Ending fade** |
| Footage edit with AI extra shots | | **Video model**, **Ending fade** |
| Dailies | | **Takes per shot** (1 to 3), **Video model**, **Ending fade** |
| Blur an object | **What to hide** (the object, in plain words), **How to hide it** (blur or pixelate) | |
| Subtitles, subtitles in another language | **Caption look**: plain, word by word, or one big word | |
| Avatar clip | **Avatar**, **Lite mode (cheaper)**, **What they do** or **What they say**, **Shape**, **Length** | **Video model**, or in Lite mode **Lip-sync model** and **Picture model** |

**Ending fade** fades the picture and the sound out at the very end, exactly as these recipes always have; switch it off
for a film that stops on its last frame. The narrated story also has it under **More options**.

A model field lists the models you can pick, the recommended one first, with the price in words compared to it
(for example "About 2x the price"). Changing a model, a length, a shape or a switch updates the price shown above
**Run**; changing text does not.
