# Move and resize on the picture

You can change where a clip, a still picture or a title sits by dragging it on the picture in the viewer, without opening the Inspector. This works on a computer and on a tablet.

## Select something on the picture

Click the picture in the viewer. The top-most clip, picture or title under the pointer is selected, and a box with handles appears around it.

- **Pick the one underneath.** Hold **Alt** (**Option** on a Mac) and click again to select the item below the first one.
- **Select nothing.** Click an empty part of the picture.
- You can click while the film is playing. The box shows an outline while it plays, and the first time you press on it the film pauses so you can drag.

The box shows whenever the playhead is on the item you selected.

## Turn the handles on or off

The **Transform** button in the tool strip above the viewer turns the box and handles on or off. It is on when you open the editor. You can also press `Shift+T`. See [Keyboard shortcuts](./shortcuts.md).

A mask, reframe, follow or corner pin tool uses the same picture. While one of those tools is on, clicking the picture does not select.

## Move

Drag the inside of the box. While you drag, the picture sticks to the middle, the thirds, the safe margins and the edges of other pictures when it gets close. A line shows what it stuck to.

To move without sticking, hold **Shift**, **Ctrl** or **Cmd** while you drag.

With the box selected you can also use the arrow keys to move it one pixel, or ten with **Shift**.

## Resize

- **Corners.** Drag a corner to make the picture bigger or smaller in any shape, so you can stretch it wider or taller.
- **Keep the shape.** Hold **Shift** while you drag a corner and the picture keeps its width-to-height shape.
- **Edges.** Drag the middle of a side to stretch the picture in one direction only.
- **From the centre.** Hold **Alt** (**Option** on a Mac) while you drag and the picture grows or shrinks around its centre.

In the Inspector, the **Transform** section shows **Width** and **Height** next to each other. Turn on **Link width and height** to change both together.

## Rotate

Drag the round handle above the box. Hold **Shift** to turn in steps of 15 degrees.

## Titles and captions

A title has the same box as a picture. Drag it to place it anywhere, drag a corner or an edge to resize it, and use the round handle to turn it. When you drag a title near one of the nine standard places (the corners, the middle of each side and the centre), it snaps there.

A caption moves between the nine standard places. While you drag, a ghost shows where it will land when you let go.

## Undo and the hint

One drag is one step, so **Undo** takes back the whole drag. The first time the handles show in a session, a short hint tells you how they work. Select **Got it** to hide it.

If a value already has keyframes, the change is saved as a keyframe at the playhead. See [Moving, scaling, rotating and fading a clip](./index.md#moving-scaling-rotating-and-fading-a-clip).

## On a phone

Tap the picture to select a clip. To move, resize or rotate it, use the clip's own controls on the phone.
