# Enhance a clip

**Enhance** improves the video of one clip. It is in the **Inspector** when you select a video clip, and in the clip sheet on a phone. You always see the result next to your original before anything changes in your timeline, and one click puts your original back.

Select a clip on a picture track, open **Enhance**, and pick what you want to do.

## What you can do

| Choice | What it does | Options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sharper picture** | Makes the picture bigger and crisper. | 2x bigger, 4x bigger, To 1080p, To 4K |
| **Smooth slow motion** | Adds in-between frames so slowed-down video looks smooth. | 2x, 4x, 8x |
| **Clean up noise** | Takes out grain and speckle from dark or low-light shots. | Light or Strong, and **Keep film grain** |
| **Motion blur** | Adds a natural blur to fast movement. | Light, Natural, Strong |
| **Extend the picture** | Adds new picture around the edges, so the frame gets bigger. | A shape, or an amount per side, and an optional description |

Sizes your plan does not include are greyed out, with a note saying what your plan allows.

## Extend the picture

Pick **Extend the picture** to make the frame bigger. Choose a shape (vertical 9:16, square 1:1, landscape 16:9 or 4:5), or set an amount for each side. You can describe what the new edges should show in plain words, up to 500 characters. This choice always uses AI, so the same consent, price estimate and steps apply. Very short clips cannot be extended, and you are told the shortest length. The result has a bigger frame and replaces your clip in one step.

## Fast or AI

**Sharper picture** and **Smooth slow motion** come in two ways:

- **Fast (free)** runs on our servers and costs nothing.
- **AI (uses credits)** usually looks better. AI slow motion goes up to 4x.

**Clean up noise** and **Motion blur** are always Fast.

Before you start, you see a line with the price (Free, or about so many credits), the size of the new video, its frame rate, roughly how big the file will be and how long it takes.

## The first time you use AI in a project

AI sends the picture of your video to an outside video service so it can be processed. The first time you choose AI in a project, ReelBolt asks you to agree. Your answer is saved for that project. Fast never sends anything and never asks.

## While it works

You see the progress and a **Cancel** button. A small **Enhancing** label also shows on the section. You can keep editing. If you cancel, nothing changes in your timeline.

## Compare, then apply

When the new video is ready, two players show **Before** and **After** over the part of the video your clip uses. Press **Play both** to watch them together.

Press **Apply** to switch the clip to the new video. If the same video is used by other clips in your timeline, they all switch together in one step, with the same length and the same trims. **Undo** puts everything back in one step, and **Use original** does the same with one click.

If the clip is no longer in your timeline when the work finishes, the result waits as **Ready - needs your OK**. Choose **Apply to this clip**, or **Keep in media only** to just keep the new file in your media. ReelBolt never applies it without you.

## From your media

In **Media**, open the menu of a video and choose **Enhance...** to run the same job on that video. The new file is added to your media, and no clip in your timeline changes.

## If something goes wrong

- **Not enough credits.** The message says how many you need, with a link to add credits.
- **Your plan does not include it.** The message links to the plans.
- **The video is too long.** The message says the longest video we can enhance.
- **You have a lot running.** Wait a moment and try again.
- **The new video came out a different length.** We do not use it, your credits are refunded, and you can try again.
