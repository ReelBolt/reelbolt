# Make a promo video with AI backgrounds

**Goal:** make a promo of your app with soft, abstract moving backgrounds behind the scenes.

## Recipe: Promo video with AI backgrounds

Template: **Promo video from your code with AI backgrounds**.

**Set before you run it.** Language, length, shape, style and notes, as for
[Promo video from your code](promo-from-code.md). The backgrounds are abstract colour and light
with no text and no people; you do not describe them. Setting meanings are in
[Run settings](run-settings.md).

**What you need first.** Your source code in the project, and a video-generation provider set up
by your admin. Without the provider the run stops at the background step.

**What it costs.** It uses credits and AI shots. Generation is real spend, limited to one or two
short clips per run and capped. The run page shows the price and the generation allowance before
you start. Your plan must allow AI shots.

**What you get.** One promo video of your app's real screens, with generated backgrounds the
animated scenes play over.

**Common problems.**

- The run stops at the background step: no video-generation provider is connected, or your plan
  does not include AI shots. Ask your admin, or use the plain promo.
- The backgrounds are not visible: they sit behind the scenes. They are subtle by design.
- You want a promo in your brand: use [Promo video in your brand](promo-in-your-brand.md).
