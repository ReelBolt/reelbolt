# Make a promo video in your brand

**Goal:** make a short promo of your app that follows your approved brand, built from a saved
design and product profile instead of your code.

## Recipe: Promo video in your brand

Template: **Promo video from your profile** (shown in the gallery as "Promo video in your brand").

**Set before you run it.** Language, length (15, 30 or 60 seconds are offered), shape, style, notes
on who it is for, and **Design**. Leave **Design** on the project's approved design to build in
your brand, or choose none to run without brand colours and fonts. Setting meanings are in
[Run settings](run-settings.md).

**What you need first.** An approved design and an approved product profile in the project. If one
is missing, the run stops at its first step with a plain message. Build the profile first; see
[Design system](../design-system.md).

**What it costs.** It uses credits, and it skips the steps that read your code, so a repeat run is
lighter than [Promo video from your code](promo-from-code.md). How much lighter has not been
measured yet. The run page shows the price.

**What you get.** One silent promo video of your app in your brand, with on-screen text and
captions written for you.

**Common problems.**

- The run stops asking for a profile: the design or the product profile is not approved yet.
  Approve both, then run again.
- The video does not look like your brand: check that **Design** is not set to none.
- The product facts are out of date: update and approve the profile, then run again.
