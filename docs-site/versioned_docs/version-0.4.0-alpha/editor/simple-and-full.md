# The simple editor and the full editor

The editor has two layouts: **Simple** and **Full**. They are two ways of looking at the same timeline.
Nothing about your video depends on which one you use: the clips, the picture in the viewer, undo and
the exported film are the same in both.

- **Simple** shows the essentials: your media, the viewer, the timeline, and one panel that follows
  what you select. It is meant for a first video and for quick changes.
- **Full** shows every page and tool: the seven pages described in [Pages and panels](pages-and-panels.md),
  with colour grading, masks, the mixer, markers, versions and the rest.

On a phone there is one layout, made for a phone, and no switch.

## Switching

The **Simple | Full** switch is at the top right of the editor, next to **Export**. Click the side you
want. The editor remembers your choice on this device.

Switching changes the layout and nothing else. Your selection, the playhead, your undo history and any
edit that is still being saved all carry over.

The first time you open the editor it picks a layout for you. A tablet starts in Simple. A computer
starts in Simple too, unless you have already changed a keyboard shortcut or saved a panel layout: then
it starts in Full, because you have set the full editor up to your liking.

## What Simple shows

### Three places

At the top left are three places. They change the panel on the right; the viewer and the timeline stay
where they are.

- **Edit**: the task list, or the clip you selected.
- **Look**: a look for the clip (Warm, Cool, Filmic and others) with how strong it is, five sliders
  (brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, tint), **Fix exposure**, **Show the original**, and
  **Apply to all clips**.
- **Export**: where the video is going (Full HD landscape, vertical for phones, square and so on),
  what it costs, the render button, and your film when it is ready.

### The task list

With nothing selected, the panel lists things people usually want done:

- **Remove the silences**: finds the quiet parts and cuts them in one step. Free.
- **Add captions**: writes captions from what is said. Reading the speech uses credits; you see the
  price before anything runs.
- **Add a title**: words on the picture, at the playhead.
- **Cut to the music**: finds the beats in your music and lines your cuts up with them. Free.
- **Fix the sound**: the volume of each track, music that lowers itself under a voice, a cleaner
  voice, and a voice-over.
- **Change the shape**: vertical, square or wide, and the frame rate.
- **Tighten the edit**: a helper suggests cuts. You see the price first and accept only the
  suggestions you like.

A task never changes your video by itself. It opens the tool that does, and every change that tool
makes is one step you can undo.

### A selected clip

Click a clip on the timeline and the panel shows that clip: **Split here**, **Duplicate** and
**Delete** at the top, then how the picture sits in the frame (Fit or Fill, position, size, rotate,
opacity), speed, volume, and fades. You can also drag the picture in the viewer to move and resize it.

If the clip has work that was made in the full editor, such as colour steps, a mask or an animation,
the panel says so in one line with an **Open in the full editor** button. That work stays in the picture
and in your export; Simple just does not have the controls to change it.

### Getting back to suggestions

The **AI tools** button at the top of the right-hand panel is always there. If you open Look or Export
and want your suggestions again, press it. What you had typed or received is still there.

### Asking for a change

Under the viewer is a line that says **Ask for a change**. Pick what kind of change it is (Edit,
Titles, Colour, Sound, or Check my video), type what you want, and press **Continue**. The panel opens
with your request filled in and shows the price. Nothing is sent, and nothing is charged, until you
press **Ask for suggestions** there. You then review each suggestion and accept the ones you want.

### The timeline

The timeline is the same one as in the full editor, with a shorter toolbar: select, blade, undo, redo,
split at the playhead, delete, snapping, and zoom. Dragging a file from **Media** onto the timeline,
moving clips, trimming their ends and deleting them all work the same way.

### A quieter viewer

The viewer shows your film with every effect, as it does in the full editor. It only tells you about
the preview when there is something to say: a badge appears when part of the picture is shown
approximately or needs a render to be exact, and a small note appears while frames are being skipped.
When neither is true, the viewer says nothing.

## Getting to the full editor

- The **Simple | Full** switch, at any time.
- **Open in the full editor** on a clip that has work made there.
- **More export options** in the Export place opens the full Deliver page: review links, timeline
  files and hand-off packages.
- The command palette lists every action. An action that needs the full editor says
  **Opens the full editor** on its row and takes you there.
- A keyboard shortcut for something only the full editor has does not switch by itself. A note offers
  **Open the full editor**, so a key pressed by accident never moves you.

## What stays the same

- **Your timeline.** There is one timeline. A video you graded and masked in Full opens in Simple and
  exports as the same film.
- **Saving.** Edits save by themselves in both layouts. The name of the timeline and **Saved** are at
  the top left.
- **Shortcuts.** The keys on [Keyboard shortcuts](shortcuts.md) work in both, except the few that need
  the full editor.
- **Prices.** Anything that uses credits shows its price first, in both layouts.
