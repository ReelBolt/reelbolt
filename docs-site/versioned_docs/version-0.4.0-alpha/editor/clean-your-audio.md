# Clean your audio

**Clean voice** makes the sound of one clip clearer. It is on the **Audio** page of the editor, and in the **Audio** sheet on a phone. You always hear the result before anything changes in your timeline, and one click puts your original sound back.

Select a clip that has sound, then pick one of the options.

## Clean the voice

- **Basic** is free. It softens steady background noise, like a fan or the hum of a room. It does not remove sudden sounds, music or other voices.
- **Studio** uses credits. It keeps the voice and removes almost everything else, even loud noise. It takes longer, and it can make a voice sound slightly processed.

## Remove a noise

Pick the one sound that bothers you.

| Option | Cost | What it does | What it does not do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hum** | Free | Removes a steady low buzz from lights or mains power. | It does not touch the voice or other sounds. |
| **Wind** | Free | Softens wind rumble on outdoor recordings. | It does not remove sharp gusts completely. |
| **Hiss** | Free | Softens the steady hiss of a quiet room or an old microphone. | It does not fix a muffled voice. |
| **Echo** | Free | Reduces the room echo that makes speech sound far away. | It does not make a bad recording perfect. |
| **Music** | Uses credits | Takes the background music out and keeps the talking. | Very loud music may leave a trace. |

If there is nothing to remove, for example no hum in the clip, the editor says so, for example "No hum found, nothing changed", and your clip stays as it was.

## How it works

1. Pick an option. The editor shows what it costs, either **Free** or "About N credits".
2. Press **Clean it**. The first time you use an option that uses credits, the editor asks if it may send the audio of the clip to an outside audio service to be processed. The audio is stored nowhere but in your project. The free options never ask. Your answer is remembered on this device.
3. Wait while the clip is cleaned. This can take a minute.
4. Listen. **Before** and **After** play the same part of your clip, so you can switch and compare.
5. Press **Use the clean audio** to put the clean sound on your timeline. This is one step, so **Undo** takes it back.
6. Changed your mind? Press **Use original** to go back to your original sound at once.

If a clip is linked to its picture, only its sound is replaced and the picture stays as it was. A picture clip with its own sound is replaced with a copy that has the clean sound.

## If something goes wrong

- **Not enough credits.** Nothing is charged. The editor says how many more credits you need and links to **Add credits**.
- **Your plan does not include this option.** The editor links to the plans.
- **Too many jobs are running.** Wait for one to finish, then try again.
- **Read-only timeline.** A timeline you can only look at cannot be cleaned.

The editor is part of the **Early preview**, so some tools may change.
