# The basics

New here? You are in the right place. These short guides take you from a new account to a finished video. Each one has numbered steps and screenshots, and takes a few minutes. Not sure what to make? Start from [What do you want to make?](../index.md#what-do-you-want-to-make).

## Your first video

1. [Sign up and take a first look](./sign-up-and-first-look.md): create your account and find your way around.
2. [Create a project and upload files](./create-a-project-and-upload.md): make a home for one video and add your material.
3. [Pick an app and run it](./pick-an-app-and-run.md): choose what kind of video to make and start it.
4. [Follow progress, then download or share](./follow-progress-and-share.md): watch the run and get your video.

Guides 1 to 4 are all you need for your first video.

## Next steps

5. [Ask for changes](./ask-for-changes.md): change part of a finished video without starting over.
6. [Editor basics](./editor-basics.md): open a video as a timeline (Early preview).
7. [Assistant basics](./assistant-basics.md): ask questions and let the assistant do things for you.
8. [Pictures and thumbnails](./pictures-and-thumbnails.md): make a picture or thumbnail (Early preview).
9. [Plan, credits and billing](./plan-credits-and-billing.md): see what you have and what work costs.

## Optional next steps

- [Connect a runner](./connect-a-runner.md): render on a computer you own instead of in the cloud.

For the full detail on any topic, use the links at the end of each guide, or see [What is ReelBolt](../index.md).
