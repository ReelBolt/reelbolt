# Review, removal and your copies

## Review

Every recipe and every new version is checked automatically. A recipe can wait for a person when the check cannot decide, when the author has not shared a recipe before and is not on a paid plan, or when it matches something that was removed earlier. A recipe waiting for review is not in the gallery.

## When a recipe is removed

The ReelBolt team can remove a recipe, and a recipe that gets several reports is hidden while it is checked. The author receives an email that names a category of reason, such as content, copyright or low quality. It does not include notes or who reported.

Copies already made stay in the projects that have them. A removed recipe cannot be copied again. An author who has had a recipe removed has later recipes reviewed by a person before they appear.

If you delete your account, your recipes are deleted with it. Copies other people made stay with them.

## Ask for a takedown

If a recipe uses your work without permission or breaks the rules, email support@reelbolt.ai. Include the link to the recipe, what is wrong, and how to reach you. You can also use the report option on the recipe. See [Rate and report a recipe](./rate-and-report-a-recipe.md).
