# Edit my video

**Goal:** turn footage into a tight edit and add only the extras you want. The result is a new MP4.

This one app replaces the older footage recipes (the plain highlight edit, the testimonial reel, titles and
callouts, music, sound effects, colour grade, extra AI shots, voice-over, fixing flubbed lines and the editing
rooms). Their old names still open this app with the matching field already set.

## Before you run it

- **Your video** uploaded to the project (Files tab).
- A chat AI provider. Music needs a music file in the project. A voice-over and fixing flubbed lines need a
  voice provider. Extra AI shots need a video-generation provider.

## The fields

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Your video** | The clips to edit. |
| **What is it** | Raw footage, a finished animation or a screen recording. It tells the editor what to look for. |
| **Length** and **Shape** | The target length and the picture shape. |
| **Sound effects** | Off, subtle or strong: hits, whooshes and clicks on the action. |
| **Captions** | Off, plain, word by word, one big word, or big and bold. |
| **Lite mode (cheaper)** | A plain cut with captions and transitions: the extras are ignored and there is no AI review. |
| **Notes** | What to keep or show. |

Under **More options**:

- **Music** (off by default) and **Your music track**: a track from the project's music files, lowered while people
  speak. "On, cutting to the beat" also lands the cuts on the beat.
- **Zooms and camera moves** (subtle by default; off, subtle or dynamic), **Speed ramps** and **Flashes** (both on by
  default): placed on the action in the picture. Switch them off if you want a plain cut. Speed ramps and flashes
  work together with zooms and camera moves.
- **Graphics** (off, titles, callouts) and **Transitions** (automatic or expressive).
- **Colour look** chosen by an AI colour team.
- **Extra AI shots**: up to four short AI shots. They are paid and have a fixed cap you see in the estimate.
- **Voice-over**, with its **language** and **voice**.
- **Fix flubbed lines**: stumbled lines are re-recorded in your project voice. Needs speech in the video.
- **Cut on the action** (on by default): cuts land on things that happen in the picture.

## What you pay for

Only what you switch on is made, held and charged. The estimate before you run shows the cost of exactly your
choices. Sending your footage to a vendor is never a field of the app.

## If something goes wrong

- **Music is on but nothing is mixed in:** add a music file to the project first.
- **Speed ramps or flashes did nothing:** switch on zooms and camera moves too.
