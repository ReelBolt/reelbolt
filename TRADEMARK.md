## What you can always do

You do not need permission to:

- **Say what you are talking about.** Write "ReelBolt" in text to refer to the project or the hosted service, for example in a review, a tutorial, a blog post, a comparison or a bug report. Use it as a name, not as part of yours.
- **Say what yours works with.** "Works with ReelBolt", "an integration for ReelBolt", "hosted on infrastructure of your choice, running ReelBolt" are fine when they are true.
- **Use the code.** Fork it, change it, run it, redistribute it under the AGPL. The licence gives you that, and this policy does not take it away.
- **Link to us.** A link to this repository or to reelbolt.ai, with the name as the link text, is always fine.
- **Keep the notices.** You must keep the copyright, licence and attribution notices that come with the code.

## What forks and derivative products may and may not do

If you publish a modified version or build a product on ReelBolt:

- **Choose your own name and logo.** Do not call it ReelBolt, and do not use a name, logo, icon, colour scheme or domain that could be mistaken for ours. Names such as "ReelBolt Pro", "ReelBolt Cloud" or "Reel-Bolt" are too close.
- **Remove the ReelBolt mark and logo** from the user interface, app icons, installers, documentation sites and marketing material of your fork. The files under [`brand/`](brand/) are in the repository so the software builds; they are licensed for that purpose and not as a brand for your product.
- **Say it is a fork, truthfully.** "Based on ReelBolt" or "a fork of ReelBolt", in plain text and in the same size as surrounding text, is fine. Do not imply that we made, endorse, support or certify your version.
- **Do not suggest an official status.** No "official", "certified", "authorised" or "by the ReelBolt team" unless we have agreed to it in writing.
- **Change the update and support pointers.** Make sure your build does not send users to our support channels, billing, accounts or update servers as if it were ours.

## Self-hosting

Running an unmodified ReelBolt for yourself, your team or your company is fine, and the name appears in the product because it is the software you installed. You may say that you run ReelBolt.

If you offer a **hosted service** to other people, make it clear that you operate it and we do not. A line like "Runs on the open-source ReelBolt software. Operated by [your company], not affiliated with the ReelBolt project." works. Do not use "ReelBolt" in the name of your service or its domain.

A "powered by ReelBolt" or "built with ReelBolt" badge for your product, your videos or your website is welcome, in plain text or with the unmodified logo from `brand/`, provided it says what it is and does not suggest that we run, sponsor or endorse you.
