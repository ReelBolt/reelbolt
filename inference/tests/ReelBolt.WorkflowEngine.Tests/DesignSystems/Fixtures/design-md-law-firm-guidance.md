# Harbor and Finch Legal

## Overview

A calm, formal identity for a boutique law firm. Authority comes from restraint: generous space, a serif voice and one deep navy accent.

## Colour palette

- Navy ink for headings and the logo mark
- Warm paper white for backgrounds
- A single brass accent for links and rules

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- ✓ Write in plain, precise English that a client can follow
- ✓ Lead every slide with the one point it proves
- ✓ Use the navy and brass palette on every surface
- ✓ Keep one idea per sentence
- ✓ Show the firm name in full on the first appearance
- ✓ Use sentence case for headlines
- ✓ Leave generous white space around the logo

### Don't

- ✗ Don't introduce colour outside the navy and white base
- ✗ Don't promise outcomes or use guarantee language
- ✗ Don't use exclamation marks
- ✗ Don't use legal jargon without a plain explanation
- ✗ Don't stretch or recolour the logo
- ✗ Don't use stock photos of gavels or scales
- ✗ Don't write in all capitals

## Voice

- Measured
- Reassuring
- Precise
