# Social publishing: draft Terms and Privacy additions

**DRAFT for owner sign-off. Not in force.** Nothing here changes the Terms or Privacy Policy pages or the `legal_documents` versions
(Terms 2026-10-26, Privacy 2026-10-31). Adding it to the Terms is a version bump and re-acceptance; to the Privacy Policy it is an
acknowledgement. Have counsel review before publishing. The wording follows the Google limited-use disclosures already made for
the Google sign-in review.

## Proposed Terms addition: "Publishing to social platforms"

> **Publishing to social platforms.** If your plan includes it, you can connect your own account on a supported platform (today
> YouTube and LinkedIn) and ask ReelBolt to post your videos there, now or at a time you choose. You authorize ReelBolt to act on
> your account only to do what you ask. You are responsible for the videos, titles, text and labels you post, for having the rights
> to post them, for correctly labelling content that is made with AI where the platform asks for it, and for following the platform's
> own terms and community rules. ReelBolt may refuse, cancel or hold a post that fails our content checks, your plan's limits or a
> platform's limits. A platform may remove content, limit visibility or suspend an account, and ReelBolt is not responsible for
> that. Until a platform has approved ReelBolt, it may keep your videos private. Posts that we cannot confirm went out are shown as
> such and are not retried automatically; you should check the platform before trying again. You can disconnect an account at any
> time. Workspace owners and admins manage connected accounts for the workspace, and members who can edit a project can post that
> project's videos to them.

## Proposed Privacy addition: "Connected social accounts"

> **Connected social accounts.** When you connect a social account, we receive from the platform an identifier, your display name and
> avatar address, and the permissions you granted, plus access and refresh tokens. We store the tokens encrypted and never show them.
> We use them only to post the videos you ask us to post and, where the platform allows, to read the view, like, comment, share and
> save counts of those posts for 30 days, so we can show you how they are doing. We do not use this data for advertising, do not sell
> it, do not use it to train models, and do not read other content in your account. The video, title, text and hashtags you choose
> are sent to the platform at posting time. We keep a record of each post (platform, time, text, status, link) for the life of the
> project and delete it with the project. When you disconnect an account we delete its tokens and its statistics immediately and ask
> the platform to revoke the grant; posts already published stay on the platform. Our use of information received from Google APIs
> adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
