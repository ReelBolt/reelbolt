# What is removed from a recipe you share

A public recipe carries the shape of your app and its settings. It never carries anything that belongs to you.

## Removed or left open

- **Identifiers.** Nothing in a recipe points back to your account, workspace, project, app or earlier runs.
- **Files.** Files from your project are removed. The copier is asked to choose their own file in that spot.
- **Voices.** Voice choices are removed. The copier picks a voice.
- **AI connections.** The connection to an AI service is removed. The copier uses their own, or the default one.
- **Keys and secrets.** None are ever part of a recipe.
- **Consent switches.** The setting that allows footage to be sent to outside services is switched off in every copy.
- **Built-in agents.** Choices of built-in agents go back to their defaults.

## Kept and shown

Names of steps, numbers and options, and the text you wrote into prompts, questions, labels, narration lines and overlay text. This text is public. It is cleaned of markup, checked automatically, and listed on the recipe page, so read it before you publish.

## Refused

A recipe is not published, and the reason is shown in words, when it:

- uses a custom agent or a custom list of skills of yours;
- points at a result of one of your runs, or at one of your editor timelines;
- uses a revision or a picture-design step;
- has a loop with more than 10 rounds or a pass mark outside 1 to 10, or a condition ReelBolt cannot read;
- is too large, or its text is refused by the automatic check.

The preview video is public too. Use one you have the right to show.
