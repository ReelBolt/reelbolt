# Languages

**Goal:** make a video work in more than one language. The result is a new MP4, and subtitles also come as SRT
and VTT files.

This app replaces the older subtitles, translated subtitles and dub recipes. Their old names still open it with
the matching fields set.

## Before you run it

- **Your video** uploaded to the project, with speech in it.
- A speech-to-text provider. A translation also needs a chat provider; a dub also needs a voice provider.

## The fields

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Your video** | The video to work on. |
| **Subtitles** | Off or burned in. The SRT and VTT files come with them. |
| **Translate the subtitles** | Turn on to translate them into the target language, burned in with the original timing. |
| **Target language** | The language of the translation or the dub. |
| **Dub** | Off, or a spoken translation that replaces the voices. The lips are not re-synced. |

Under **More options**: the dub voice, the spoken language and notes.

## What you pay for

Transcription is always used. The translation and the dub voice are used only when you turn them on, and the
estimate shows exactly that.
