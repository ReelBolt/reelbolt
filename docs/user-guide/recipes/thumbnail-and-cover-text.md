# Make a thumbnail with cover text

**Goal:** get a cover picture from your video, with an optional title on it.

## Recipe: Thumbnail and cover text

**Inputs.** One video in the project (**Your video**). **Which frame**: **Pick the best one**, or **Use a time I choose** and then set **Time in the video**. **Cover text** is optional, up to 90 characters.

**What it costs.** Very few credits. No AI model runs and nothing is sent to a provider, so no permission to send media applies. Only the processing time of looking at your video and grabbing the frame is charged, and you see the figure before you run it.

**What you get.** One picture saved in the project.

**If it fails.**

- The time is past the end of the video: pick a time inside its length.
- The cover text is rejected: shorten it to 90 characters or fewer.
- The picture is not the frame you wanted: choose **Use a time I choose** and set the time.
