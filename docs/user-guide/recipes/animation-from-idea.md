# Make an animated video from an idea

**Goal:** get a short animated video from a few sentences, when you have no footage and no code.

## Recipe: Animated video from your idea

**Inputs.** **Your idea**, **Language**, **Length** (10, 15 or 30 seconds), **Shape** and **Tone**. Under **More options** you can pick the models that write the scenes and build the video. No provider for video generation is needed, so every plan can run it.

**What it costs.** It uses credits. The price shows on the run page before you start, and nothing is spent until you press Run. A short video costs less than a long one.

**What you get.** One silent animated video of text, shapes and colour, saved in the project. It uses only what your idea says: it does not invent names, people, logos or numbers.

**If it fails or looks plain.**

- Say more in **Your idea**: the exact words to show, the order, and the feeling.
- Try a shorter **Length** first, then a longer one.
- Want footage of a real scene instead? See [AI video clip from your idea](ai-clip-from-idea.md) (needs a plan with video generation) or [Edit my video](edit-my-video.md).
