# Your daily video limit

Generated video is the one thing ReelBolt lets you cap per day yourself. You set the limit; nobody else's number applies to you. By default there is **no daily limit**: only your credit balance and each step's own **Max Spend** cap apply.

## Set, change or remove it

1. Open **Settings → Usage**.
2. In the **Daily video limit** card, type the most **credits** your runs may spend on generated video per day.
3. Optionally type an **Alert at** level, below the limit.
4. Select **Save**. **Remove limit** clears both and goes back to no limit.

The card shows what your runs have spent on video today and what is left. The day is the UTC day and starts over at 00:00 UTC.

## What counts

- Only generated video from runs **you started**. Another member's runs have their own limit.
- Music and sound effects, editing and other steps never count toward it.
- A run that would pass the limit stops **before** buying anything, so you are not charged. The failure says: "This needs about 120 credits of video generation but your own daily video limit is 300 credits and 290 are already used today (10 credits left)...", with where to change it.
- No plan sets a daily video budget: only your limit, the step's cap, your credits and the installation's safety ceiling apply. Runs started by a schedule have no personal limit.

## The alert

When today's spend reaches your **Alert at** level, the card and the cost line on the run page say so. The alert is shown in the app only; ReelBolt does not send an email for it.

## Where you see it

The run page's cost line shows your limit, what is used and what is left for a workflow that generates video, and the assistant reports the same when you ask about a run. See [Reading the estimate](./reading-the-estimate.md) and [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting.md).
